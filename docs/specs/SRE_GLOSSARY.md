# SRE Glossary v1.0

Purpose:
Define the official terminology used across the entire SRE project.
This document is the single source of truth for all specifications, reports, benchmarks, prompts, and documentation.

---

## Evidence

Information directly supported by an attributable primary source.

## Fact

A verified statement supported by evidence without interpretation.

## Reasoned Inference

A logical conclusion derived from verified facts through explicit reasoning, but not directly stated by the source.

## Hypothesis

A plausible explanation requiring future verification.

## Unknown

A question that cannot be answered with sufficient evidence.

## Constraint

A limiting condition whose change alters system behavior or industry structure.

## Bottleneck

The limiting factor that constrains value creation or system performance.

## Scarcity

A capability or resource that is limited relative to demand.

## Value Creation

The generation of new economic value within a system.

## Value Migration

The movement of economic value from one participant, capability, or business model to another.

## Value Capture

The sustainable retention of a disproportionate share of the value created.

## Direct Beneficiary

A participant receiving value directly from the industry change.

## Indirect Beneficiary

A participant benefiting through downstream or secondary effects.

## Confidence

The assessed strength of a conclusion based on evidence quality and reasoning.

## Verification

The process of confirming a conclusion using future evidence.

## Falsification

The conditions or evidence that would invalidate a conclusion.

---

Rules

- One definition per term.
- No examples.
- No implementation details.
- No discussion.
- Consistent terminology only.
- This glossary overrides conflicting terminology elsewhere.
