# Research OS

## Mission

Build a continuously learning strategic research system that transforms primary-source events into validated strategic knowledge through disciplined reasoning and real-world verification.

---

## Principles

1. **P0 First** — Only primary, attributable sources may found a thesis. Secondary material may verify outcomes later; it may not invent the claim.

2. **Facts Before Reasoning** — Observable facts are recorded and cited before interpretation. Reasoning adds no new facts.

3. **Unknown Remains Unknown** — Absence of evidence is an explicit output, not a prompt to speculate. Unsupported certainty is a defect.

4. **Reality Is The Final Evaluator** — Structural clarity at T0 is not predictive power. Later dated evidence confirms, weakens, or falsifies the thesis.

5. **Build Assets, Not Documents** — Reports, validations, knowledge units, and benchmarks accumulate. Disposable commentary does not.

---

## Workflow

```text
P0 Sources
    ↓
SRE
    ↓
Strategic Research Report
    ↓
Quality Gate
    ↓
Prediction Validation Framework (PVF)
    ↓
Strategic Knowledge Base (SKB)
    ↓
Future Strategic Research
```

Each stage has a frozen contract. Downstream stages cite upstream artifacts; they do not rewrite them.

---

## Governance

The system may evolve only when supported by evidence.

Changes are allowed only if at least one of the following is true:

- Multiple real cases expose the same methodological defect.
- Benchmark repeatedly identifies the same failure mode.
- Prediction Validation demonstrates a persistent forecasting failure.

Otherwise:

**Maintain stability.**

---

## Long-Term Assets

Research OS continuously accumulates four permanent assets:

| Asset | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **SRE Reports** | T0-locked strategic theses from P0 evidence |
| **Prediction Validations** | Dated tests of whether those theses held |
| **Strategic Knowledge** | Reusable, leveled lessons extracted from completed cycles |
| **Benchmarks** | Repeatable scoring of reasoning fidelity and failure modes |

These assets are append-oriented. Invalidated claims are marked superseded, not erased.

---

## Success

Research OS succeeds not by generating more reports, but by producing more validated strategic knowledge over time.
