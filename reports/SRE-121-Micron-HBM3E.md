## 1. Executive Summary

Micron announced it has begun volume production of its HBM3E (High Bandwidth Memory 3E) solution. Micron states its 24GB 8-high HBM3E will be part of NVIDIA H200 Tensor Core GPUs scheduled to begin shipping in the second calendar quarter of 2024. Micron claims ~30% lower power versus competitive offerings, bandwidth above 1.2 TB/s, and pin speeds exceeding 9.2 Gb/s. Micron also states it is sampling 36GB 12-high HBM3E for March 2024. *(Evidence-supported)*

**Key conclusion.** If generative AI systems are constrained by memory bandwidth, capacity, and power at the HBM layer, value pressure moves toward suppliers that can ship HBM3E into named AI GPU platforms. Micron is a named volume producer for NVIDIA H200 in the T0 release. Price, ASP, margin, shipment volume, and share versus other HBM suppliers are **Unknown**. *(Reasoned Inference, Medium; Unknown for magnitude)*

**Tier assignment.** Micron Technology, Inc. (NASDAQ: MU) is Tier 1 for the disclosed HBM3E supply position into H200. Magnitude of capture is Unknown.
